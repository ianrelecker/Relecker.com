FieldMark Support

FieldMark is an on-device iOS field log. Photograph issues, mark them up, and share a PDF. There are no FieldMark accounts.

Contact

Email ian.relecker@icloud.com with the device model, iOS version, and FieldMark version from Settings.

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel a personal FieldMark Pro subscription in iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions. Organization licenses bought in Apple Business Manager are assigned and revoked by an administrator. Restore Purchases in FieldMark Settings after assignment.

Data

Logs, photos, and reports stay on the device. Deleting the app deletes that data. FieldMark does not offer cloud backup.
